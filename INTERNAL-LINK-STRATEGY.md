## 1. Executive Summary & Architecture Overview

The House Robotics website employs a **Hub-and-Spoke Topical Architecture** designed to maximize link equity distribution, establish topical authority across both service verticals and content silos, and guide search engine crawlers seamlessly from high-level pillars down to transactional conversion points.

### Architecture Levels:
1. **Tier 1 (Core Pillar Hub):** Homepage (`/`) — passes top-level PageRank to major service silos and foundational trust pages (`/about`, `/contact`, `/services`, `/blog`).
2. **Tier 2 (Service Category Hubs & Flagships):** High-intent service flagships (`/seo`, `/local-seo`, `/social-media-marketing`, `/ppc-google-ads`, `/ai-automation`, `/web-development`).
3. **Tier 3 (Specialized Practice Spokes):** Deep vertical service pages (`/services/wordpress`, `/services/shopify`, `/services/ecommerce`, `/services/email-marketing`, `/services/content-marketing`, `/services/cro`, `/services/lead-generation`, `/services/analytics`, `/services/branding`, `/services/video-marketing`, `/services/online-reputation`).
4. **Tier 4 (Topical Editorial Engine):** Blog articles (`/blog/future-of-ai-marketing-2025`, etc.) linking contextually to relevant Tier 2 & Tier 3 transactional services and the `/contact` conversion endpoint.

## 4. Best Practices & Rules Followed
1. **Descriptive Anchor Text:** No non-descriptive anchors (such as "click here" or "learn more") are used as primary links. Every anchor communicates clear topical relevance.
2. **Topical Relevance:** Links are only constructed between logically adjacent verticals (e.g., SEO connects to Local SEO and Web Dev; PPC connects to CRO and Paid Social).
3. **No Orphan Pages:** Every indexable page on the site is linked from at least 3 distinct internal sources (Global Navigation, Complementary Services Carousel, Footer, or Blog Posts).
4. **Crawl Depth Limitation:** No indexable page is deeper than 2 clicks from the homepage.
